# Propose, accept and reject nooq suggestions

An agent's change becomes a nooq suggestion (DM-6), attributed to that agent. Accepting or rejecting uses nooq's acceptTransaction and rejectTransaction as the owner, and a stale suggestion is refused the way nooq's evaluateSuggestion says.
